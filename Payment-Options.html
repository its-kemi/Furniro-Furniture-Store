Payment Options

At Furniro, we want your shopping experience to be simple, convenient, and secure. We offer several payment options to make it easier for you to complete your order.

Credit and Debit Cards

You can pay for your Furniro order using an accepted credit or debit card. Please make sure that your card information is entered correctly when completing your order.

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • Other supported cards

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery may be available for selected locations and orders. When this option is available, you can pay the required amount when your order is delivered.

Availability may depend on your delivery location and order conditions.

Bank Transfer

For selected orders, customers may be able to pay through a bank transfer. If this option is available, the required payment information will be provided during the order process.

Please keep your payment confirmation until your order has been successfully processed.

Online Payment

Furniro may provide secure online payment options depending on your location and available payment services.

Always check your order details before confirming your payment.

Secure Payment

We care about the security of your payment information. Please make sure that you only enter your payment details through the official Furniro checkout process.

Important Information

Payment methods may vary depending on your location, order value, delivery method, and other applicable conditions.

Before completing your purchase, carefully review the total amount, delivery information, and selected payment method.

If you have a question about a payment or an order, please contact our support team.
