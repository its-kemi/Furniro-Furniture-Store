Payment Options

At Furniro, we offer several convenient and secure payment options to make your shopping experience simple and comfortable.

Credit and Debit Cards

You can pay securely using your credit or debit card at checkout.

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • Other supported cards

Cash on Delivery

Pay for your order in cash when your furniture is delivered to your address.

Bank Transfer

You can transfer the payment directly to our bank account using the provided bank details.

Online Payment

Use available online payment services to complete your order quickly and securely.

Secure Payment

Your payment information is handled securely. We take reasonable measures to protect your information during the payment process.

Important Information

Payment methods may vary depending on your location and order. Please make sure that your payment details are correct before completing your purchase.
