Returns & Exchanges

At Furniro, we want you to be happy with your purchase. If your order does not meet your expectations, you can review our return and exchange information below.

Return Period

Customers may request a return within the applicable return period after receiving their order. Please contact Furniro as soon as possible if you would like to return an item.

Return Eligibility

To help us process your return quickly, returned items should generally meet the following conditions:

  • The item should be unused and in good condition.
  • The original packaging should be kept whenever possible.
  • The item should not have been damaged through improper use.
  • Proof of purchase or order information may be required.
  • Some products may have specific return conditions.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or different from what you ordered, please contact our support team as soon as possible.

Please provide your order information and a clear explanation of the issue so that our team can help determine the appropriate solution.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an eligible item for another product, please contact our support team first. Exchange availability may depend on product availability, condition, and other order conditions.

Refunds

Once a returned item has been received and reviewed, we will determine whether it qualifies for a refund.

The timing of a refund may depend on the payment method and the processing time of the applicable payment provider.

Items That May Not Be Returnable

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, customization, condition, or other applicable restrictions.

Before returning a product, please contact our support team if you are unsure whether your item is eligible.

How to Request a Return

01

Contact Us

Contact Furniro support and provide your order information.

02

Review

Our team will review your request and provide the next steps.

03

Return

Follow the provided instructions to return the eligible product.

04

Resolution

After inspection, the applicable refund or exchange process will be completed.

Important Information

Return conditions may vary depending on the product, order, delivery location, and applicable policies. Please contact us before sending an item back so that you can receive the correct return instructions.

Need Help With a Return?

If you have questions about a return, exchange, or refund, our support team is ready to help.
